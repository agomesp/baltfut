# Simulation Engine & On-Field AI Realism — Deep-Dive

## Where the current engine stands

`src/lib/subs-draft/match-sim.ts` (~673 lines) is already a competent, frame-rate-independent 2D engine: momentum steering with capped accel, a possession FSM (`dribble/pass/loose/shot/attempt`), rating-driven pass error, pressing (nearest-2 pressers), a `separate()` step (Reynolds separation), through-balls with a real offside check (`offsideAt`), corners/throw-ins/goal-kicks/free-kicks + a wall, blocks/parries/rebounds, and cards → send-offs. The scoreline is authoritative from `tournament.ts::simulateMatch` (Poisson goals from XI strength) and `scoreFor()` scripts the goal. The tests only guarantee "the ball roams end-to-end and possession flips" — so there is wide latitude to raise realism without breaking contracts.

Two structural truths shape every recommendation. **(1) It runs entirely client-side inside a rAF loop** (`pitch-view.tsx`), so anything I add is free on GitHub Pages but pays a per-frame CPU cost on the viewer's phone, and freezes when the tab is backgrounded. **(2) The cosmetic sim and the authoritative Poisson result are two disconnected models.** The single biggest believability gap is not the on-field AI — it is that `simulateMatch` produces goals/shots/cards from a coin-flip Poisson while the pitch shows unrelated play. Aligning those two is the highest-leverage work here.

## Ranked upgrades

### 1. Utility-scored decision-making to replace the `Math.random()` FSM branches — S/M, client-only
`decide()` currently picks shoot/pass/cross/dribble via hard-coded probabilities (`shootP`, `passProb`, magic `0.18`). Replace each option with a **utility score** and pick softmax-weighted: `shoot = f(xG(dist,angle,pressure))`, `pass_i = teammate_openness − intercept_risk + progression`, `dribble = space_ahead − pressure`, `cross = width × box_targets`. This is exactly how New Star and simplified FM decision layers work, and how StatsBomb's **possession-value / xT** grids score actions. **Why it helps:** decisions become legible ("he shot because he was 8m out and unmarked" vs "he shot because RNG"), and quality emerges from context rather than a flat dice roll. **Effort S–M** (it's a refactor of one function, ~80 lines, into a `scoreOptions()` helper — very TDD-friendly). **Trap to avoid:** don't go full GOAP/behaviour-tree here — for one ball-carrier deciding among ~5 actions, a utility function is strictly simpler and just as expressive; GOAP's planning payoff needs multi-step goals this sim doesn't have.

### 2. A real expected-goals (xG) model shared by BOTH the cosmetic sim and the authoritative result — M, client-only (optionally Supabase)
Build one `xgOf(shotX, shotY, defendersInCone, keeperAngle)` function (logistic on distance + angle, the standard StatsBomb-style shot model). Use it in two places: (a) `decide()`'s shoot utility (#1), and (b) **replace the raw Poisson in `simulateMatch`** — accumulate per-possession chances into an xG total, then sample goals from that. **Why it's the top realism win:** right now a 92-rated squad and its opponent generate goals from `0.35 + 2.4*(s/total)` with zero shot geometry, and the watched play is decorative. A chance-based model makes the *outcome* believable (favourites still lose to a smash-and-grab, scorelines cluster like real football: many 1-0/2-1, rare 6-0) **and** lets the pitch drive the score instead of faking it. **Effort M** because you must decide the sim's authority model — simplest: keep `tournament.ts` authoritative but derive its goals from the same xG curve, so both models agree by construction. **Quick win inside this:** even without wiring the live sim, swapping Poisson-λ for an xG-shot-count model in `simulateMatch` alone makes brackets feel real.

### 3. Formations that actually shape movement + a compact block with lines of engagement — S, client-only
`target()` already nudges by role, but off-ball shape is loose. Add three team-level scalars derived from game-state: **defensive-line height**, **compactness** (vertical spread), and **width**. Have every off-ball player steer toward `formationAnchor + ballInfluence`, then clamp into the current block. Add a **line of engagement**: press only when the ball crosses a trigger y-line, otherwise hold shape (mid-block). This is the core of FM's tactical model and Google Research Football's role system. **Why:** the current pressing is "nearest 2 chase always," which reads as chaos; a holding block that *then* springs a press looks unmistakably like real defending, and makes the offside trap (already coded) visibly meaningful. **Effort S** — it's mostly reshaping `target()` and gating the presser selection on a y-trigger. **Big immersion-per-hour ratio; do this early.**

### 4. Match-state / momentum model driving tactics — S, client-only
The sim receives `progress` (0..1) and already fatigues players, but tactics never react to the score. Feed the **live scoreline** (from crossed goal events) into the team scalars: a trailing team late (`progress>0.7`, chasing) pushes its line up, commits an extra man forward, presses higher; a leading team drops deeper and slows tempo ("game management"). **Why:** end-of-match desperation and shutting up shop are the most recognizable "this is a real match" beats. **Effort S** — a few multipliers keyed off `hg-ag` and `progress`. Pairs naturally with #3.

### 5. Steering-behaviour polish: arrive/pursue + cohesion, better ball-carrier evasion — S, client-only
The engine has seek + separation. Add Reynolds **pursue** (lead the ball's future position — partly done via `ball.vx*0.12`), **arrive** (already present as the `d<5` slowdown), and light **cohesion** toward the unit centroid so lines don't get strung out. Give the carrier a small "steer away from nearest presser" vector so dribbling looks intentional. **Effort S.** **Why:** removes the residual "dots teleport toward the ball" feel. **Tradeoff:** cohesion can over-clump — keep its weight below separation's.

### 6. Calibrated event-rate distributions (pass %, shots, duels, fouls) — S, client-only
Tune constants so aggregate stats land in real ranges: pass completion ~78–88%, ~10–14 shots/side, ~1.3 fouls→card ratio, corners ~5/side. The HUD already shows possession + shots, so miscalibration is *visible*. Add a post-match assertion test that 100 sims stay within these bands (you already have the Poisson + `range()` test harness). **Effort S.** **Why:** believability is judged partly on the stat line a viewer can read, not just motion. This is a **quick win** and guards regressions.

### 7. Richer set-piece routines & scripted goal variety — S/M, client-only
`scoreFor()` always tucks the ball into a corner "past a beaten keeper." Add a small library of goal *types* chosen by build-up context: header from a cross, tap-in from a cutback, long-range screamer, penalty, deflection. Similarly, add near/far-post corner variants and short-corner routines (partly present). **Why:** repetition is the immersion-killer once viewers watch several matches together. **Effort S–M.** **Trap:** keep goals consistent with the authoritative scorer's role (`SCORER_WEIGHT` already weights strikers) — a defender's scripted goal should be a set-piece header, not a solo run.

### 8. Deterministic seeded replay + server-authoritative shared sim — M, needs Supabase/cheap server
Because subs watch *together*, everyone should see the **same** match. Make the sim deterministic from a seed (replace `Math.random()` with a seeded PRNG, e.g. mulberry32) and broadcast `{matchId, seed, authoritativeResult}` over **Supabase Realtime**. Each client runs the identical sim locally — no per-frame server cost, perfect sync, and it fixes the "everyone sees different play" problem. **Why:** shared truth is the difference between "a screensaver" and "we're watching the game." **Effort M**, and the seed/broadcast rides your existing Realtime + Edge Function stack — no new host needed. This also enables the background-tab fix: on tab-refocus, fast-forward the deterministic sim to `serverClock` instead of freezing.

### 9. In-browser ML for movement (TF.js / onnxruntime-web) — L, assess-only, likely a TRAP
Imitation-learning or RL policies (à la Google Research Football's PPO agents) *can* produce lifelike off-ball runs, but for this project the effort/payoff is poor: you'd need trajectory training data (StatsBomb 360 / SkillCorner-style, which you don't have), a training pipeline, and a 1–5MB ONNX model shipped to phones running 22 inferences/frame in `onnxruntime-web`. **Verdict:** not worth it versus utility-AI (#1) which gets 80% of the believability for 5% of the effort. The one *cheap* ML angle worth prototyping in `experiments/`: a tiny offline-trained logistic **xG model** exported as plain coefficients (not a runtime tensor) — that's just #2 with better-fit weights, no runtime ML dependency.

## Summary ordering
Do **#2 (shared xG model)** and **#3 (real formation block + engagement line)** first — together they fix both *outcome* realism and *play* realism, the two explicit goals. Then **#1** (utility decisions) makes it legible, **#4/#6** (match-state + calibrated rates) make it feel like a real 90 minutes, and **#8** (seeded shared sim over Realtime) makes it a *shared watch*. Treat **#9** as a parked experiment. Files to touch: `match-sim.ts` (#1,3,4,5,7), a new `xg.ts` shared by `match-sim.ts` + `tournament.ts` (#2,6), `pitch-view.tsx` + a Realtime channel (#8).
